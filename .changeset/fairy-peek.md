Look into things in the Fairy chat by resting the pointer on them. A conversation in the workspace sidebar or the history shows how it stands, its last few messages, the last steps Fairy took and an approval it waits on. A helper's task card shows what the helper said last and the steps it took. A folded tool call shows what went in and what came out, and a run of folded calls lists each one with what it worked on and how it went. The new `fairy.preview` action gives that glance at one conversation.
