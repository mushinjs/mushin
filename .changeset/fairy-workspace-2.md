The dashboard's **Fairy** button opens the Fairy workspace, with a band of light sweeping across it now and then; in the workspace the same spot holds **Console**, which goes back to the page you left. The buttons that collapse the sidebar and show or hide Fairy beside the page are in the top bar. In the workspace, either side folds away when dragged half its minimum width past the narrowest and opens again when dragged back out, the status panel shows colored dots instead of status tags when narrow, and Skills and Fairy settings open in a dialog.
